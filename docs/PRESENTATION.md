## Slide 1: Title Slide

- **Presenter**: Project Team

---

## Slide 2: Problem Statement & Motivation
- **Challenges**:
  - Manual inventory tracking leads to unexpected stockouts and project delays.
  - Lack of supplier performance history leads to poor vendor choices.
  - High material wastage without scrap or defect tracking.
- **Solution**: Centralized digital platform powered by AI procurement assistance and automated stock control.

---

## Slide 3: Key System Features
- **Material Master**: Reorder points, storage rules, reserved stock, unit valuation.
- **Procurement Pipeline**: Purchase Requests → Purchase Orders → Goods Receiving Notes (GRNs) → Quality Inspections.
- **Stock Management**: Inventory transactions, warehouse transfers, scrap logging, supplier returns.
- **AI Intelligence Suite**:
  - Demand & Depletion Forecasting
  - Supplier Recommendation & Lead-Time Prediction
  - In-Stock Substitute Suggestions
  - Wastage Anomaly Detection
  - Natural Language Chatbot Assistant

---

## Slide 4: Technology Stack
- **Backend**: Python 3.12+, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy ORM, Pydantic v2, PyJWT, bcrypt.
- **AI/ML**: Scikit-Learn (Linear Regression), Google Gemini LLM API.
- **Database**: MySQL / PostgreSQL relational database.
- **Frontend**: HTML5, CSS3, Bootstrap 5, Vanilla JS ES6+, Chart.js, FontAwesome.
- **DevOps**: Docker, Docker Compose, Swagger/OpenAPI (`/docs`).
